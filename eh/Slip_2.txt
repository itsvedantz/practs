Question A:
Implement an inverted index construction algorithm for the following two documents:
document1 = "The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog"
document2 = "The lazy dog slept in the sun"
Build a simple document retrieval system using the constructed index to find the documents containing terms “lazy sun”.

Answer A:
import re

docs=[
"The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog",
"The lazy dog slept in the sun"
]

index={}

for i,d in enumerate(docs):
    words=re.findall(r'\w+',d.lower())
    for w in words:
        index.setdefault(w,set()).add(i+1)

query=["lazy","sun"]
res=set(index[query[0]])

for q in query[1:]:
    res=res.intersection(index[q])

print(index)
print(res)


Question B:
Write a program to calculate precision, recall and F-measure where true positive is 60, false positive is 30 and false negative is 20.

Answer B:
tp=60
fp=30
fn=20

precision=tp/(tp+fp)
recall=tp/(tp+fn)
f=2*(precision*recall)/(precision+recall)

print("Precision:",precision)
print("Recall:",recall)
print("F-measure:",f)
