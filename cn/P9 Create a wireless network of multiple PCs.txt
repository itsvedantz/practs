Add a wireless interface to each PC as follows:
1.Click on the PC and open it.
2.Go to the Physical tab.
3.Switch OFF the power of the PC.
4.From the modules list, select PT-HOST-NM-1W.
5.Drag and insert the module into the empty slot of the PC.
6.Switch ON the power again.

Adding IP Address to each PC
1) PC0:
IPv4 Address: 10.0.0.1
Subnet Mask: 255.0.0.0
2) PC1:
